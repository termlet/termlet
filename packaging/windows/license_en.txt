4. Data and privacy
Your hosts, credentials and other data are stored on your computer and never pass through our servers. By default, the Software sends anonymous usage statistics at most once a day (such as its version, the operating system, CPU architecture, interface language and which features were used; never hosts, commands or files, and nothing that identifies you or your computer). The receiving server also works out your country or region from your network address, without storing the address. You can turn this off in Settings at any time. It contacts the update server when checking for updates. To prevent abuse, Cloudflare rate-limits requests by network address; blocked requests may briefly appear with their address in Cloudflare's security logs. Termlet itself does not read or store addresses. Other features that use the network, such as cloud sync and sending crash reports automatically, are used only after you turn them on. See https://termlet.dev/en/privacy/ for details.
